Factorio Mod to move resources and researches from Fulgora and Vulcanus to Nauvis.\
\
Changes:\
    - Fulgora and Vulcanus no longer exist. Nauvis mostly untouched. Aquilo and Gleba untouched.\
    - Only Calcite, Scrap and Tungsten Ore spawn on Nauvis. Lava, Oil Ocean and Sulfuric Acid Geysers removed completely.\
    - No longer needed, thus removed: Foundation, Lightning Collector, Lightning Rod, Molten Copper from Lava, Molten Iron from Lava, Rail Support Foundations and Steam Condensation.\
    - Surface Conditions removed from Fulgora and Vulcanus exclusives.\
    - Default import location from Fulgora and Vulcanus items changed to Nauvis.\
    - Recycling, Calcite Processing and Tungsten Carbide prerequisites changed to Purple Science, LDS, Blue Circuits and Concrete.\
    - Recycling and Tungsten Carbide triggers techs changed to mine one Scrap and one Tungsten Ore respectively.\
  Balancing:\
    - Removed Acid Neutralisation.\
    - Recycling yields only Holmium Ore.\
